## Draw defect found and repaired during stage 2 — 〜を通じて + 〜を通して

**The defect.** The seed's first draw put BOTH `〜を通じて` (grammar_p7[3] → 問34)
and `〜を通して` (grammar_p7[7] → 問38) in one paper. They are two separate,
UNTAGGED entries in `pools.json`, so `check_spec_grammar_form_families` was
structurally silent — this is exactly the debt the two coverage WARNs on this
paper describe (問題7 was 1/12 family-tagged), and it is the first time that debt
produced an actual collision rather than a theoretical one.

**The evidence they are ONE grammar point**, measured from the primary authority
(the 31-sitting archive), not asserted from memory or from a textbook:
- Across the **20 sittings** whose `booklet.md` contains either form, **no
  official item anywhere offers 〜を通じて and 〜を通して as two options of the
  same item.** You cannot key one against the other.
- Where official DOES put one in an option set, the rivals are unrelated forms:
  7/2012 keys 「を通して」 against 「を問わず／にかけて／にもかかわらず」; 12/2022
  contrasts 「を通じて」 with 「に向けて」.
- 12/2024 uses **both** forms in running prose in the same medium/experience
  sense, in one sitting — i.e. they are interchangeable there.

**The repair, which is the sanctioned one and not a hand-substitution.**
`AGENTS.md` §0's founding incident is a QA fix that hand-substituted instead of
rerolling; the stage-2 author had instead honoured the draw and separated the two

items on one grammar point, which `exam-qa-review` §3 "One grammar point, one KEY

   `"媒介"` (9 → 11 tagged entries). **No prior paper drew both** (checked across
   all 29 specs: 20260810_1 and 20260821_1 took 〜を通して alone; 20260812_1 and
   20260828_1 took 〜を通じて alone), so this tag retro-FAILs nothing. **Pool
   depth is unchanged at 172**, so no `cooldown_for()` window moved and open item
   #1's retro-FAIL mechanism is not engaged.

**The new adjacency this creates, which QA should check rather than assume away.**
「〜にかけては」 is the superlative "as for / when it comes to" sense, but bare
「〜にかけて」 is a period-span form, and 問34 keys 〜を通じて in the *period* sense.
Official 7/2012 put を通して and にかけて in ONE option set precisely because they
compete on that axis. The 文法 author was sent back with instructions to force
問38's superlative reading unambiguously, to keep にかけて/にかけては out of 問34's
options and を通じて/を通して out of 問38's, and to re-angle 問34 to the medium
sense instead if the period reading cannot stand cleanly beside it.
