## RC-4 — the 分裂文 template regex cannot see 「べきは」 (QA round-1 F2's root cause) — NOT APPLIED

**The rule.** `FINAL_SENTENCE_TEMPLATES`' 分裂文 row is spelled
`(の|ん)は、?[^。]{2,60}(だ|である|…)。?\s*$`. A cleft closing built on
「〜べきは…である」 — or 「ことは」「ものは」「点は」「側は」 — is the same sentence
pattern and the regex cannot see it. Round-1 F2 was exactly that: three surfaces
on the cleft (問題10(5), 問題11(4), 問題12(A)) while the gate printed 1.

**Status: the paper is repaired (問題11(4) re-closed, wide count now 2 = cap),
but the GATE is not.** The next paper can reproduce F2 with the gate green.

**Why the widening was not applied on 2026-09-21, stated honestly.** It needs a
retro-impact measurement and the one I ran is REFUTED by its own output. I
widened the pattern to `(のは|んは|ことは|べきは|ものは|点は|側は)` and counted
paragraph-final sentences across all 39 papers. It reported `20260917_1` at **3**
where round-2 QA — using the closing column properly — measured **2**, and it
fired on `imported-n2-2021-12` (3) and `imported-n2-2023-07` (4), which
contradicts the 0–2 official band the rule is calibrated against. The predicate
is counting every paragraph-final sentence in the 問題9–13 region, not the
**13 surface closings** that `dokkai_closing_scopes()` enumerates. A gate change
justified by that number would be the REPORT-GOI §F10 defect class — a threshold
set from an unreproducible measurement.

**What the repair actually needs** (for whoever picks this up):
1. Count with `dokkai_closing_scopes()`, the module's OWN enumeration of the 13
   closings, not an ad-hoc paragraph scan. That function exists precisely so this
   denominator cannot drift.
2. Re-measure all 39 papers on that scope, and the 10 official imports
   separately — the official band is what makes the cap defensible.
3. Only then widen the row, and grandfather whatever the measurement names.
   Round-1 QA reported that only `20260807_1` (already in
   `FINAL_TEMPLATE_GRANDFATHERED`) and the then-unrepaired `20260917_1` reached 3
   on its widened predicate; that figure is plausible but was NOT reproduced here
   and should be re-derived rather than trusted.

**Blocking status: this row is OPEN and blocks the next generation run** until
applied or rejected with a reason, per `AGENTS.md` §0.
