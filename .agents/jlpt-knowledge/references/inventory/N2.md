## Rules for batch authors (read first)

- **`page` = PDF page** (the drill schema). The printed page goes in `note` (「本のp.N」).
  The Shin Kanzen 文法 offset is **not constant**:
  - 問題紹介 +11
  - 第1部 1–26課 and IV +10
  - 第2部 +9
  - 第3部 +8, then +10 (PDF 166 is a duplicate scan)
  - 模擬試験 and 索引 +9

  Every `pdf_page` in the inventory was read off the scan, not computed. Other
  books drift too (SK 読解, SK 語彙, SK 漢字); their offsets are in the `books`
  and `sources_structure` blocks.
- `official.sittings_tested` counts distinct sittings (of 31) where the form is:
  - in the 問題7 key,
  - in 問題8 (stem or cards), or
  - in a 問題9 key.

  **Do not copy it into `official_count`** — batch-0 QA found it wrong for 8 of
  20 points even at `ok` (false hits like ありがたい→がたい, plus misses); `ok`
  means the search was specific, not that each hit was read. Confirm hit by hit
  (jlpt-knowledge SKILL §Quiz integrity rule 4). Historically: use it only when `count_confidence` is `ok`. Generic forms
  such as だけ, とは, として, ぐらい, によって and ものだ are flagged `low`; trust
  their `p7_key` count only.
- Same-form clusters share one surface form, so author each cluster in one batch
  and disambiguate by meaning:
  - ものだ: 23-1 / 24-3 / 26-2
  - ことだ: 24-2 / 26-5
  - ものか: 12-3 / 24-6
  - まい: 22-4 / 24-5
  - どころではない: 12-2 / 18-5
  - といったら: 13-3 / 13-5
  - となると: 13-4 / 15-1
  - にしても: 15-6 / 19-6 / 10-3
- The ▲ cautions in `caution_paraphrase` are the transcribers' own paraphrases. No
  textbook or exam sentence was transcribed.
- **`group` labels carry the part prefix**, because 第2部 and 第3部 reuse 課 numbers:
  - 「第1部 18課」, 「第1部 IV-D」, 「第3部 6課」
  - 「敬語」, 「副詞（呼応）」, 「助詞」, 「話し言葉」, 「SK外（公式・プール）」

  Batch 1's existing 「第N課」 labels should be migrated.
- IDs are suggestions (`g-…` romaji, `v-NNNN` = Hajimete number, `k-NNNN` = SK
  kanji number). The 20 written entries keep their current IDs.
- **Every 文法 row carries `book`** = `[section, lesson, item(, split)]`, its Shin
  Kanzen position (目次 PDF 3–7, IV tables PDF 132–145; jlpt-knowledge SKILL §Book
  order). Copy it into the entry unchanged; it decides where the card is listed
  and which `group` label is right. Splitting a row by meaning adds a 4th element
  and a categories.json `book_splits` line. 2026-09-30: `g-joken` moved to
  「第3部 3課」 (条件を表す文, PDF 168–169 teaches と・ば・たら・なら・のなら・であれば),
  and row `g-to-iu-koto-ni-naru` (結論; SK IV-D lists ということだ as 伝聞 only)
  was added to SK外 — 294 rows.
